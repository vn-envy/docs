Narad is a personal AI assistant that runs entirely on your laptop or desktop — no cloud dependency, no data leaving your machine. Modelled on the divine messenger Narad Muni, who holds the Mahati veena and decides which string to pluck for each task, the system routes your requests to one or more specialist agents (called **Avatāras**), runs them in parallel or sequence, and synthesises their outputs into a single coherent reply. Every conversation, every learned pattern, and every audit record lives in `~/.narad/` on your own disk.

## Why Local-First?

Running on your own machine means your calendar data, finance records, health logs, and conversation history never touch an external server. Narad binds to `127.0.0.1` by default, so nothing is reachable from the network unless you explicitly choose to rebind. All embeddings, memory vectors, and learned patterns are stored in `~/.narad/` using local SQLite and LanceDB — the moat is the compounding relationship, not a subscription wall.

## Architecture at a Glance

Every message you send travels through this pipeline before a response reaches you:

```
You → Narad (supervisor) → 1–3 Avatāras (specialists)
           ↑                        ↓
      Smriti (recall)        Tapas (score → Sutra)
      Sutras (inject)        Yantra (trace)
      Sankalpa (style)       Karma (audit)
```

Narad reads your full conversation history, selects the right avatāras, and synthesises their outputs into one reply. Smriti recalls relevant past conversations before each run. Tapas scores the response afterwards and promotes learned patterns. Karma records every action verdict to an immutable audit log.
