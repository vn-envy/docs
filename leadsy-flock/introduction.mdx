Leadsy Flock is an AI growth agency built for neighbourhood shops across India — gyms, cafés, bakeries, boutiques, clinics, salons. You paste a Google listing URL you own, approve a campaign brief and price, and the Flock's AI agents produce a complete, bilingual advertising kit using your shop's own photos and videos. You always make the final click; Leadsy Flock never autoposts on your behalf.

## Why Leadsy Flock

Every neighbourhood shop deserves advertising that looks like a national brand produced it. Leadsy Flock closes that gap by combining Google's Gemini, Veo 3.1, and ADK agents with a safety-first pipeline that puts your shop — not invented stock footage — at the centre of every frame.
