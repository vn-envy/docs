When you approve a campaign, Leadsy Flock starts six sequential pipeline steps, each running asynchronously in the background. Every step records a status receipt when it finishes. The pipeline is designed to be safe-by-default: no Veo or Gemini Image calls happen before your YES, and every step is idempotent so a retry never double-spends.

## Pipeline steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scout — Brand and market intelligence">
    **Agent:** Scout  
    **Input:** Business listing URL, Google Maps link, website URI, campaign brief  

    Scout uses Gemini with Google Search and Maps to read everything publicly available about your shop. It builds a `BrandSpec` — a structured document containing your brand's colour palette, tone, theme ID, and a list of your own photo and media URIs (`ownUris`). Scout also gathers local competitor insights and builds a shelf of comparable ads in your category and city.

    **Output:** `BrandSpec` (theme, palette, tone, ownUris, competitor insight, ad shelf)

    **On failure:** The pipeline stops. The step receipt records `status: error` with the failure reason. No subsequent steps run. You can trigger a retry from the run room.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Inka — Creative production">
    **Agent:** Inka  
    **Input:** `BrandSpec`, campaign brief, `ownUris`

    Inka is the artist. It generates bilingual copy in English and your local Indic language, then starts two 8-second Veo 3.1 films: a **place film** showing your interior or storefront, and a **proof film** showing your dish, product, or result. Inka uses your own listing photos as Veo reference frames — no invented UGC, no stock models. It also requests a 2-second Lyria instrumental audio sting (no vocals). Inka starts Veo and returns immediately; the harvest step polls for completion.

    **Output:** Copy variants (EN + Indic), Veo job IDs, Lyria job ID, Gemini Image still (if no usable shop photo exists)

    **On failure:** The pipeline stops. Inka will not substitute invented imagery for missing shop photos — if `ownUris` is empty, it falls back to a Gemini Image still generated from your brief, not from scraped stock.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Harvest — Video polling and assembly">
    **Agent:** Inka sidecar  
    **Input:** Veo job IDs, Lyria job ID

    Harvest polls Veo 3.1 until both 8-second clips (place + proof) are complete, then polls Lyria for the audio sting. Once all media is ready, ffmpeg derives five aspect ratios from the Veo 9:16 master: 4:5 feed, 1:1 square, 9:16 story, 1.91:1 landscape, and 16:9 for the landing page. Audio is muxed onto each clip in both English and Indic VO tracks.

    **Output:** All media slots populated — stills, clips, and multi-ratio derivatives ready for kit assembly

    **On failure:** Harvest retries on a backoff schedule. If Veo times out, the receipt records `status: polling` and the step remains open until the clip arrives or the campaign expires.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Creative Gate — Safety review">
    **Agent:** Ledge  
    **Input:** All copy variants, media slot paths

    Ledge runs a three-layer safety check on every piece of copy:

    1. **Regex pass** — Instant block on hard-coded banned strings
    2. **Gemma classifier** — Lightweight on-device model scores copy for prohibited claims
    3. **Gemini judge** — Full model review for edge cases that pass the first two layers

    Ledge blocks copy containing: "guaranteed", "miracle", "cure", "100%", "clinically proven", medical claims, or any unsubstantiated superlative. All user-submitted text is also sanitised through Model Armor before it reaches any model.

    **Output:** `pass` or `blocked_creative` status per copy variant

    **On failure (blocked):** The receipt records `status: blocked_creative` with the failing clause highlighted. The pipeline pauses. You can edit the flagged copy and resume from the gate step.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stella — Consent landing page">
    **Agent:** Stella  
    **Input:** Approved creative, BrandSpec theme, campaign brief

    Stella builds the consent-first landing page at `/l/{campaign_id}`. The page uses your BrandSpec theme tokens (inkstone, ember, grove, slate, or paper) and displays your hero image, the place film, and an opt-in form with an explicit checkbox. The checkbox is required — Stella's template does not allow the form to submit without `consent: true`. The landing page is WCAG AA compliant; all colour tokens are pre-validated against contrast ratios.

    **Output:** Live landing page at `/l/{campaign_id}`

    **On failure:** The pipeline stops. The kit will not assemble without a valid landing page, because the kit's UTM links point to it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ad Kit — Final assembly">
    **Input:** All approved media slots, copy variants, UTM-tracked landing URL

    The final step assembles all six channel variants into the paste kit at `/k/{campaign_id}`. Each variant includes the headline (character-capped per platform), primary text, CTA, localised copy in English and Indic, UTM-tracked URL pointing to the Stella landing page, still path, clip path, proof clip path, pixel dimensions, and a lint check result.

    **Output:** Complete paste kit at `/k/{campaign_id}`, `autopost: false`

    **On failure:** The kit assembles partially and marks incomplete slots. You can access completed variants while missing slots finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step status values

Every pipeline step records a status as it progresses. You can read step status directly from the run room or via `GET /v1/campaigns/{id}`.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `started` | The step has begun processing |
| `ok` | The step completed successfully |
| `polling` | The step is waiting on an async job (Veo, Lyria) |
| `blocked_creative` | Ledge blocked one or more copy variants at the Creative Gate |
| `error` | The step encountered an unrecoverable error |

<Note>
  The pipeline is idempotent. If a step already shows `ok`, it is skipped on retry — you are never charged twice for the same step. To force a step to re-run (for example, if you want new copy after a `blocked_creative`), pass `forceRetry: true` when calling `POST /v1/campaigns/{id}/approve` again.
</Note>
