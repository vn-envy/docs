Your finished ad kit at `/k/{campaign_id}` contains everything you need to run ads across Meta, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Google — with no design work left for you to do. Each channel variant is pre-sized, pre-captioned in English and your local Indic language, and linked to the Stella landing page with UTM tracking already baked in. You open the platform, paste the copy, and upload the file.

## Channel variants

Every kit contains six channel variants. Each is independently ready to upload; you do not need all six to run a campaign.

| Slot | Platform | Aspect Ratio | Pixel Dimensions | Format |
|------|----------|-------------|-----------------|--------|
| `meta_feed` | Meta Feed (Facebook + Instagram) | 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 | Still + 8s clip |
| `meta_square` | Meta Carousel | 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 | Still + 8s clip |
| `meta_reel` | Meta / Instagram Reels | 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 | 8s clip |
| `whatsapp_status` | WhatsApp Status (organic) | 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 | Still + 8s clip |
| `google_display` | Google Responsive Display Ads | 1.91:1 | 1200 × 628 | Still |
| `google_rsa` | Google Search | Text only | — | Headline + description |

## Creative assets

### Veo 3.1 films

Inka produces two 8-second cinematic films for every campaign:

- **Place film** — Shows your interior, storefront, or shop environment. Conditioned on your own listing photos so the space looks recognisably like your shop.
- **Proof film** — Shows your dish, product, treatment result, or key offering. Uses your own product or menu photos as reference frames.

Both films are generated at native 9:16 (1080 × 1920). ffmpeg then derives four additional ratios from each master:

| Derivative | Ratio | Method |
|------------|-------|--------|
| Feed clip | 4:5 | Centre crop, scaled to 1080 × 1350 |
| Square clip | 1:1 | Centre crop, scaled to 1080 × 1080 |
| Landscape clip | 1.91:1 | Centre crop, scaled to 1200 × 628 |
| Landing / in-stream | 16:9 | Centre crop for Stella hero |

No second Veo call is made for aspect ratio derivatives — one master, five crops.

### Lyria audio sting

Each kit includes a 2-second instrumental music sting generated by Lyria. The sting has no vocals and is muxed onto the clip variants. Both VO tracks (English and Indic) are also available as separate clip files: `clip-en` and `clip-indic`.

### Still images

Every clip slot has a matching still image at the same dimensions. Stills come from your own listing photos, cleaned and framed by Gemini. A Gemini Image still is generated from scratch only when your listing has no usable photos.

## Bilingual copy

Every copy element in the kit ships in two languages: English and the local Indic language for your shop's area. For a shop in Gurgaon that is Hindi; for a shop in Mumbai it would be Marathi; and so on.

Each copy field is character-capped per platform requirements:

| Field | Meta Feed | Google RSA |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| Headline | 40 chars | 30 chars |
| Primary text | 125 chars | — |
| Description | — | 90 chars |
| CTA | 20 chars | 15 chars |

Copy variants are lint-checked automatically. The kit shows a green tick next to any variant that passes and a warning flag on anything approaching the character limit.

## UTM tracking

Every URL in the kit points to your Stella landing page (`/l/{campaign_id}`) with UTM parameters pre-populated per channel:

```text
https://your-flock-domain/l/{campaign_id}
  ?utm_source=meta
  &utm_medium=paid
  &utm_campaign={campaign_id}
  &utm_content=meta_feed
```

You do not need to edit these links. Paste the URL from the kit directly into the platform's destination URL field.

## Media slot URLs

You can access individual media files directly via the media API:

| URL | Returns |
|-----|---------|
| `GET /media/{id}/still` | Campaign hero still image (16:9) |
| `GET /media/{id}/ready` | JSON map of which slots are ready |
| `GET /media/{id}/still-feed` | 4:5 still (1080 × 1350) |
| `GET /media/{id}/still-square` | 1:1 still (1080 × 1080) |
| `GET /media/{id}/still-story` | 9:16 still (1080 × 1920) |
| `GET /media/{id}/still-landscape` | 1.91:1 still (1200 × 628) |
| `GET /media/{id}/clip` | 9:16 master clip |
| `GET /media/{id}/clip-feed` | 4:5 clip (1080 × 1350) |
| `GET /media/{id}/clip-square` | 1:1 clip (1080 × 1080) |
| `GET /media/{id}/clip-story` | 9:16 clip (1080 × 1920) |
| `GET /media/{id}/clip-en` | Clip with English VO track |
| `GET /media/{id}/clip-indic` | Clip with Indic VO track |

## How to upload to each platform

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Meta Feed">
    1. Open Meta Ads Manager and click **Create**.
    2. Select your campaign objective (Traffic or Engagement for most local shops).
    3. At the ad level, choose **Single image or video**.
    4. Upload the `meta_feed` still or clip from your kit.
    5. Paste the `meta_feed` headline, primary text, and CTA from the kit.
    6. Paste the UTM-tracked URL into the Website URL field.
    7. Click **Publish**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Instagram Reels">
    1. Open Meta Ads Manager and select **Instagram** as your placement.
    2. Choose **Reels** under Instagram placements.
    3. Upload the `meta_reel` 9:16 clip.
    4. Paste the Reels caption from the kit (English or Indic — your choice).
    5. Add the UTM URL as your destination.
    6. Publish.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="WhatsApp Status">
    1. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
    2. Tap **Status → Add to my status**.
    3. Upload the `whatsapp_status` still or clip.
    4. Add the primary text from the kit as your status caption.
    5. Tap **Send**. (This is organic — no UTM required for WhatsApp Status.)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Display">
    1. Open Google Ads and create a **Display** campaign.
    2. At the ad level, choose **Responsive display ad**.
    3. Upload the `google_display` 1.91:1 still (1200 × 628).
    4. Paste the `google_rsa` headline and description from the kit.
    5. Add the UTM URL as your final URL.
    6. Save and publish.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Search">
    1. Open Google Ads and create a **Search** campaign.
    2. At the ad level, choose **Responsive search ad**.
    3. Paste each of the `google_rsa` headlines (up to 15) from the kit.
    4. Paste the descriptions (up to 4).
    5. Set your final URL (the UTM-tracked link is in the kit).
    6. Save and publish.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Start with `meta_feed` and `google_rsa` if you are running your first campaign. These two slots cover the widest reach with the least setup effort. Add Reels and WhatsApp Status once you are comfortable with the workflow.
</Tip>
