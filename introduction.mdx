KritXlabs is a portfolio of five AI-native products built for developers, operators, and individuals who want AI that works reliably in the real world. Each product solves a specific problem — from giving your AI agent persistent, queryable memory to helping a neighbourhood shop owner produce a professional video ad in minutes. All five products are open-source under the Apache-2.0 license, and every design decision prioritises transparency, local ownership, and measurable quality over black-box convenience.

## Choose Your Product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify your use case">
    Browse the [Products](/products) page for a structured overview of all five tools. Each entry describes what the product does, who it is for, and its key capabilities — so you can find the right fit in under two minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the product's quickstart">
    Every product has its own Quickstart guide. Head to the product's introduction page (linked in the cards above), then follow the step-by-step setup. Most products are running in under five minutes on a local machine with no cloud account required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore deeper guides and the API">
    Once you have the product running, explore the Concepts and API Reference sections in its sidebar. Each product ships with example notebooks, integration guides, and reference documentation so you can go from prototype to production with confidence.
  </Step>
</Steps>
