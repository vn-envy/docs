Getting your first Rolecrft report takes about five minutes of setup and another few minutes for the AI to run its six analysis stages. You'll walk away with an 8-category resume score, AI-edited resume with tracked changes, ATS keyword scan, action plan, STAR interview stories, tailored interview questions, and networking scripts — all in one dashboard built for your specific target role.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rolecrft">
    Navigate to [rolecrft.app](https://rolecrft.app) in any modern browser. You'll land on the input screen where you provide your application details. No account or sign-up is required to get started.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste or upload your resume">
    Provide your resume in whichever format you have it. Rolecrft accepts three input methods:

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Paste text">
        Copy your resume text and paste it directly into the resume field. This works for any format and gives you the most control over what gets submitted.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Upload PDF">
        Click **Upload** and select a PDF file. Rolecrft extracts the text client-side. Complex multi-column PDFs occasionally lose formatting during extraction — check the preview before continuing.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Upload DOCX or TXT">
        Upload a `.docx` (Microsoft Word) or `.txt` file. Rolecrft converts Word documents to plain text before processing.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    <Tip>
      Uploading a PDF is convenient, but pasting text gives the most reliable extraction — especially for resumes with tables, icons, or multi-column layouts. If your PDF preview looks garbled, switch to paste mode before proceeding.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your target company, role, and JD text">
    Fill in four fields:

    - **Target company**: The company you're applying to (e.g., "Stripe", "Zepto").
    - **Target role**: The exact job title from the posting (e.g., "Senior Product Manager – Payments").
    - **Job description**: Paste the full job description text. Include the responsibilities, requirements, and preferred qualifications sections — Rolecrft uses every part of the JD.
    - **Years of experience**: Select the band that matches your total years in the relevant field.

    The more complete your JD text is, the more accurate your ATS keyword scan and interview question bank will be.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the free preview">
    Rolecrft runs three analyses immediately without charging you:

    1. **Company research**: Public information about the company's products, culture, and recent news.
    2. **JD parsing**: The skills, responsibilities, and seniority signals extracted from your job description.
    3. **Preliminary resume score**: Your ATS and hiring-manager scores with a Cleared / Conditional / Grounded status.

    Review the preliminary score and the parsed JD to confirm Rolecrft has understood your application correctly before paying.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Unlock the full report ($15 / ₹199)">
    Click **Unlock Full Report** to proceed to payment. The full report runs six AI analysis stages in sequence:

    1. Resume transformation with [KEPT]/[IMPROVED]/[ADDED]/[SUGGESTED] tags
    2. ATS keyword scan with one-click injection
    3. Prioritised action plan with score impact estimates
    4. STAR interview stories from your actual experience
    5. Interview question bank tailored to your role and company
    6. Similar companies and networking outreach scripts

    Payment is per analysis — you pay once for this specific resume + JD combination.

    <Note>
      Rolecrft stores your last 3 analyses in your browser. You can switch between them from the history panel at the top of the dashboard without re-paying.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore the 4-tab results dashboard">
    Your full report opens in a four-tab dashboard. Each tab covers a distinct part of the application process:

    | Tab | Contents |
    |-----|----------|
    | **Runway Check** | Your 8-category score breakdown, dual ATS/HM scores, and Cleared / Conditional / Grounded status |
    | **Hangar** | AI-edited resume with change tags, ATS keyword scan, and one-click keyword injection |
    | **Flight Plan** | STAR interview stories plus interview question bank with coaching strategies |
    | **Launch Pad** | Prioritised action plan (quick_win / medium_term / strategic), networking scripts, and 5 similar companies |

    Use the **Export Report** button at the top right to download the full report as an HTML file you can open in any browser. Export STAR stories separately as a CSV from the **Flight Plan** tab. To export your transformed resume as a print-ready document, use the export option in the **Hangar** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>
