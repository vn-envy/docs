Rolecrft is an AI career intelligence app built for job seekers who are serious about a specific role at a specific company. You paste your resume and the job description, name your target company and role, and Rolecrft runs six AI analysis stages to produce a complete job application toolkit — an 8-category resume score, AI-edited resume with tracked changes, ATS keyword scan, prioritised action plan, STAR interview stories, tailored interview question bank, and networking outreach scripts. A single full analysis costs $15 (or ₹199 in India).

## What Rolecrft Produces

Rolecrft delivers four tabs of output for every full analysis. Each tab covers a distinct stage of the application process, with networking scripts and similar companies consolidated alongside your action plan in the final tab.

| Tab | What's Inside |
|-----|--------------|
| **Runway Check** | 8-category score breakdown, dual ATS/HM scores, and Cleared / Conditional / Grounded status |
| **Hangar** | AI-edited resume with [KEPT]/[IMPROVED]/[ADDED]/[SUGGESTED] change tags and one-click ATS keyword injection |
| **Flight Plan** | STAR interview stories from your real experience plus a tailored interview question bank |
| **Launch Pad** | Prioritised action plan, networking outreach scripts, and similar companies to target in parallel |

## Who Rolecrft Is For

Rolecrft is built for mid-career and senior professionals applying to roles where the competition is high enough that an optimised application makes a real difference. It's particularly useful if you're:

- Switching domains or industries and need to reframe existing experience
- Targeting a company where you need a referral to get past the ATS
- Preparing for a structured interview process with behavioural and technical rounds
- Applying from outside the target market (e.g., India to US, or vice versa) and need to localise your resume

<Note>
  Each analysis is per job application — one resume plus one job description produces one complete toolkit. You can store up to 3 past analyses in your browser's local storage and restore them with a single click.
</Note>
